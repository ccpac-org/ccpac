The Achievers' Blueprint

"The Achievers' Blueprint" is a special publication and interview series initiated by the Chittagong College Physics & Astronomy Club (CCPAC). This platform is curated based on the specific strategies, preparation methods, and insights of our talented college students and alumni who have proven themselves on national and international Olympiad stages.

It highlights specific action plans for representing the country on the international arena, practical experiences of successfully balancing academic studies with an Olympiad journey, and provides a clear blueprint of success and guiding discussions for budding science enthusiasts.
