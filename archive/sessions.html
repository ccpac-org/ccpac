SESSIONS

Academic Sessions

CCPAC regularly conducts academic sessions on physics and astronomy, led by seniors, alumni, and invited instructors. These sessions cover a wide range of topics, from fundamental concepts to advanced research, providing members with insights into higher studies, scientific thinking, and real-world applications. Active participation is encouraged, allowing students to ask questions, engage in discussions, and explore ideas beyond textbooks.

Olympiad Sessions

To prepare members for local, national, and international physics competitions, CCPAC organizes dedicated Olympiad Sessions. These sessions focus on problem-solving techniques, conceptual clarity, and analytical skills. Members receive guidance from experienced seniors and alumni who have excelled in various Olympiads, helping them develop a competitive edge and deepen their understanding of physics.
