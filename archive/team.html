LEADERSHIP & ADMINISTRATION

CCPAC Team

The CCPAC Team is embodied by our dynamic Executive Panel—serving as the core administrative and leadership backbone of the Chittagong College Physics & Astronomy Club. Assumed for a fixed tenure each year, this dedicated body takes charge of steering the club's vision, governance, and structural framework.

Operating under the direct direction and supervision of the honourable club moderators and college administration, the Executive Panel manages overall club operations, strategic planning, and institutional coordination. They act as the primary bridge between general members, alumni, and governing authorities to ensure seamless administrative execution.

Through structured teamwork and disciplined leadership, the Executive Panel maintains organizational excellence. Their commitment ensures that every administrative tier functions harmoniously, cultivating a robust environment dedicated to scientific inquiry, institutional discipline, and long-term community building.
